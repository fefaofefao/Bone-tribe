# Store listing — English (en-US)

**Title (≤30):** Bone Tribe: Skeleton Roguelike

**Short description (≤80):** Build Bonesy's body from the bones of the monsters you defeat!

**Full description (≤4000):**

Bonesy woke up at the bottom of the Forgotten Crypt with a problem: only the skull is left. To get out, Bonesy has to build a brand-new body from the bones of every monster defeated along the way.

Each run takes a few minutes and ends with a one-of-a-kind creature. Bat wings, scorpion tail, spider legs: everything you attach shows up on the body and changes how you fight.

🦴 BUILD YOUR BODY
• 7 slots: skull, ribs, two arms, legs, back and tail
• 67 bones from 6 monster families: more than 10 options for every body part
• Match pieces from the same family to awaken forms like the Night Manticore
• Discover 3 secret forms… and watch out for rejection!

📜 SHORT STORIES
• 60 hand-written events full of humor and mystery
• Choices that depend on the body you built
• Chests that bite, spiders that clap and a Bone Hunter on your trail

⚔️ AUTO-BATTLER, ONE-HANDED PLAY
• Tap, choose and watch Bonesy fight
• 14 monsters, 3 bosses and a secret at the bottom of the crypt

🏛️ PROGRESSION
• Ossuary with permanent upgrades
• Bone collection, companions and relics
• Cabinet of Curiosities with 30 items to collect
• 7-day and 28-day login calendars

📸 SHARE YOUR CREATURE
At the end of every run, the game creates a Creature Card ready to post. Can you build something worse?
